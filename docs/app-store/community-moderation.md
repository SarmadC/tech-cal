# Community moderation operations

KureCal combines pre-submission content filtering with user reporting, blocking,
and an administrator review queue.

The pre-submission filter is a narrow first-line safeguard, not comprehensive
automated moderation. Reporting, blocking, and timely human review remain the
authoritative controls for objectionable content and abusive behavior.

## Response targets

- Credible threats, child exploitation, or imminent safety issues: review immediately and within 4 hours.
- Hate, harassment, or sexual content: review within 24 hours.
- Spam, misinformation, and other reports: review within 2 business days.

## Review procedure

1. Open the community report queue and inspect the reported item, surrounding context, and prior reports.
2. Remove content that violates the Terms, restrict the profile when necessary, and record a concise resolution note.
3. Escalate credible safety threats to the designated safety contact and applicable authorities where legally required.
4. Preserve only the minimum audit information required for abuse prevention and legal obligations.
5. Test the report, block, and moderation queue flows before each App Store release.

The support contact published in App Store Connect and in the app must route to a
monitored inbox. Operational ownership and the escalation contact must be assigned
before the production launch.
